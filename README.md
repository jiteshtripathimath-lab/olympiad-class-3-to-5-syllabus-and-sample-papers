## 📖 About This Archive

This repository hosts open-access syllabus decompositions, high-order thinking skills (HOTS) questions, and printable OMR guides for school students appearing in national and international Olympiads:
* **SOF IMO** (International Mathematics Olympiad)
* **SOF NSO** (National Science Olympiad)
* **SOF IEO** (International English Olympiad)
* **SilverZone IOM & IOS**
* **Unified Council NSTSE & UIMO**
