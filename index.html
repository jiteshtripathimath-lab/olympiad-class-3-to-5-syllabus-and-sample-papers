⭐ High Order Thinking Skill (2 Marks)

A clock shows 3:15 PM. If the minute hand is rotated 180 degrees clockwise, what new time does the clock display?

A) 3:30 PM
B) 3:45 PM (Correct Answer) ✓ Correct
C) 4:00 PM
D) 9:15 PM
Explanation: At 3:15 PM, the minute hand points directly at the numeral 3 (15 minutes). A 180° rotation moves the hand exactly opposite, pointing at numeral 9 (45 minutes). Therefore, the time becomes 3:45 PM.
